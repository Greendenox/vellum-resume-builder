Privacy

Your resume stays yours.

Vellum is local-first. There is no account, no resume database, and no analytics.

The short version: your resume content is saved in this app on your device. We do not sell it, train on it, or share it. The only optional outbound request is a job-posting URL you choose to import.

What is stored on your device

  • Resume content you enter: contact details, summary, experience, education, skills, projects, and certifications.
  • Your template, accent color, theme, active resume, and onboarding state.
  • Your local Pro launch-preview state.

This data is kept in the app's local storage. It leaves the device only if you export a JSON backup or save a PDF yourself.

Job URL import

If you choose Role Studio → Import from URL, the URL is sent to a third-party reader service (currently Jina Reader, with AllOrigins as a fallback) to retrieve the public posting text. Do not import confidential, internal, or authenticated postings. You can always paste the description instead.

Imported text is processed in the app. Vellum has no server that receives it.

Microsoft Store purchases

If a future version offers a paid Pro plan, Microsoft would process the purchase and provide the app with entitlement status only. Microsoft's privacy statement would apply to the transaction. The current launch preview unlocks Pro free and does not collect payment.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

No analytics SDK, advertising SDK, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking is included. We do not collect telemetry.

Your controls

  • Export: File menu → Export JSON backup.
  • Delete: File menu → Clear all local data.
  • Avoid external fetch: use Paste text instead of URL import.
  • Browser storage: clear site data for Vellum to remove local resumes.

Security and children

Data is protected by your Windows account and device security. Anyone with access to your unlocked device and browser profile may be able to view it. Vellum is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email support@comwil.ch or use the support page. Do not include a full resume or sensitive personal information in a public support request.
